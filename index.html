Rust Strength

iPhone app · Available now

A workout tracker that tells you what weight to put on the bar for your next set. Log weight, reps, and how hard it felt, and Rust Strength estimates your strength and suggests your next load. Track streaks and records, train with friends, and keep logging even without signal.

Shelfsmith

iPhone app · Open beta

Knows what food you have and tells you what you can cook with it. Scan a grocery receipt to fill a shared household pantry, then see recipes you can make right now and ones you are one or two items short of, with calories and macros.

Shelfsmith is in open beta on TestFlight. Join to try it early and help shape it.
