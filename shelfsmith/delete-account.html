Delete your Shelfsmith account

Shelfsmith is made by Bluestem Software LLC. You can delete your Shelfsmith account, and the personal information that goes with it, at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Shelfsmith and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@bluestemsoftware.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Shelfsmith account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, use the email address that account shares with us. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We then delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it is done.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Shelfsmith Plus subscription, because Apple or Google bills it. Cancel it first, or you will keep being charged. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, password and links to Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your preferences, favourites and privacy choices.
  • Diets and allergies, if you stored them.
  • Notification tokens and your time zone.
  • Your subscription status. We also ask RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, to delete your customer record.
  • If you delete your account in the app and you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect Shelfsmith from your Apple ID. For a request by email, remove Shelfsmith yourself on your iPhone in Settings, under your name, then Sign in with Apple.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Shared household data. Pantry items, list entries, meal plan entries and cooking history you added stay with your household, because the other members rely on them, but they are no longer linked to you. If you are the household's last member, the household is deleted with all of its data.
  • Backups and logs. Our hosting provider keeps backups and server logs for a limited period, normally no longer than 30 days, after which deleted information is overwritten.
  • Purchase records. Apple or Google keeps its own records of purchases under its own privacy policy.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove some of your information. In Settings, under Privacy, withdraw permission for Health information to delete your diets and allergies. You can also leave your household in Settings. You can also email us to ask us to delete specific information.
